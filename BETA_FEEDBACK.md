# Data Stories — Beta Feedback Questions

Use these questions in Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, or an email to testers. Aim for a five-minute response.

1. How easy was it to get started? (1 very difficult – 5 very easy)
2. Did the Discover questions seem relevant and interesting? (1–5)
3. Did Investigate Further help you notice something you hadn't considered? (Yes / Somewhat / No)
4. Were the charts and plain-language explanations understandable? (1–5)
5. What was the most useful discovery or feature?
6. What confused you or didn't work? Please describe the steps if you found a bug.
7. What would you most like the app to do next?
8. Optional: What kind of data did you try? (Do not submit the underlying data or any personal information.)

Suggested invitation:

> I'm testing an early version of Data Stories, a website that helps people explore their own CSV data without specialized technical skills. Could you try the sample dataset or upload a non-sensitive CSV, explore a discovery or two, and share a few minutes of feedback? Please don't upload private or personal information.
